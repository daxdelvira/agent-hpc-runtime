\section{Conclusion}

Agentic steering removes the phase predictability that byte-oriented staging
depends upon, and systems built on that assumption fail structurally rather
than through insufficient accuracy. The unit of work is also misplaced: for
these workloads the dominant cost of making data usable is a representation
change performed within a live process, and that structure and a parked
model's weights are the same class of object---anonymous memory drawn from a
single allocation. Managing them against one budget, rather than as two tiers,
is what renders the residency decision expressible.

\sysname{} makes that decision by ranking residency on stall avoided per
gigabyte held and restricting speculation to memory the arbitrator has not
claimed, reducing end-to-end wall time by 34.5--50.8\% relative to a runtime
that retains nothing and by a further 10.3--17.1\% relative to a baseline that
already retains both resource classes.
